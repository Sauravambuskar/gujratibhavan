Gujarat Bhavan is a Gujarati dharamshala (pilgrim guest house) at 15/16, Sannadhi Street, Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu 623526. It is managed by Shri Dakshin Bharatiya Gujarati Seva Trust and offers AC and non-AC rooms for families, couples and groups, plus an in-house Bhojanalaya serving pure vegetarian Gujarati food without onion and garlic.

Gujarat Bhavan is on Sannadhi Street in the temple area of Rameswaram, a short walk from Sri Ramanathaswamy Temple and Agni Theertham. It is a convenient base for darshan, the 22 theertham snan and local sightseeing.
