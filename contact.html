How to reach

Getting to Gujarat Bhavan, Sannadhi Street.

Gujarat Bhavan is in the Ramanathaswamy Temple area of Rameswaram. Autos are easily available across town — ask for “Gujarat Bhavan, Sannadhi Street”.
